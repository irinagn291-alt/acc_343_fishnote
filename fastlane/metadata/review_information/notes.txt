No system permissions are requested at launch or during use, and no permission usage-description strings are supplied. There is no login, in-app purchase, account deletion flow or App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ads and analytics are absent. User-created content is present as private pet names, care-round names and care records; public posting, sharing feeds, messaging and other social user-generated content are absent.

Fishnote records daily pet-care routines for a household with one or more animals. Users name their pets, arrange each pet’s care rounds, and use a token to record completed care, review daily history, and track consecutive complete days.

From a cold launch on a new device installation, the introduction opens after a possible brief loading view. Tap “Continue” on “A round for every pet.”, then “Continue” on “Beckon. Then hand.”, then “Start caring” on “A small record of care.” to open “Today”. “Skip” also opens “Today”. No credentials or demo account are needed. Tap “Add a pet”, enter a name in “Name” under “Pet name”, leave “Include in daily care” enabled, and tap “Save pet”. Tap “Add care round”, enter “Care round name”, choose the “Pet”, set “Order” to a whole number of zero or greater, leave “Include every day” enabled, and tap “Save care round”. Use “Close” to return to “Today” if needed. Tap “Beckon” for a pet, then “Hand” to record its next unfinished round. Alternatively, drag the token to the selected pet. Select a pet again after each successful round. “Give token” to another pet records a miss and does not complete care or change the selection. “Undo” reopens today’s latest eligible round. Logging every due round completes the day automatically.

From “Today”, open “Pets” to edit pets and “Routines” to edit care rounds. Tap “Open the care log” to open “Care log”; at larger accessibility text sizes the control is labelled “Care log”. Tap “Continue today's care”, or “View today's care” when complete, to return. “How care works” opens the guide. Open “Settings” for “Read the introduction” and “Reset all data”. Later launches return to “Today” with saved information. A fresh simulator installation skips the introduction and includes “Mabel”, “Otis” and “Pip”, three prior complete days, and “Mabel” selected with no rounds logged today; normal device installations have no starter content.

Household data and care history stay on this device; “Contact Fishnote” in “Settings” opens an external contact page, and core use requires no network connection.

There are no region-based content changes or restrictions; dates and formatted numbers follow device regional settings, and daily boundaries and streak calculations use the device’s current calendar and local time. No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies to the described personal pet-care log because it provides no medical advice, health assessment or product-health information. The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations; there are no health citations. There is no barcode or QR-code scanning.
